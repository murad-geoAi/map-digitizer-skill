FIRM Panel Review

How to use this

  • Drag a red GCP marker if it's placed at the wrong real-world location.
  • Click a green zone polygon or blue BFE line to edit its label, or drag its vertices to reshape it.
  • Use the draw toolbar (top-left of the map) to add a missing GCP / zone / BFE line, or to delete one.
  • Click Save & Rebuild when done - this refits the georeferencing, rewrites the GeoTIFF, and rebuilds the GeoPackage.

GCP residuals

Large / flagged residuals usually mean a misread street name or bad geocode match, not real map distortion - check that point first.
